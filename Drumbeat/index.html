Program

Imagine a folk festival with many different tents — tents for all different sorts of music, a tent where you learn how to fiddle, and maybe one where you make drums.

The Drumbeat Festival will work exactly like this. We'll have all kinds of spaces where you can teach and learn — studios, labs, playgrounds.

Schedule

Want to know exactly what's going to happen? You can see the draft schedule here on the wiki.

Festival Spaces

Here's some of the different spaces we're planning for the Drumbeat Festival.

Badge lab

Test, critique and improve badges and tools that recognize informal online learning.

Webcraft toolshed

Get your hands dirty testing and improving standards-based web developer courses.

Peer-learning fishbowl

Provide feedback on peer-to-peer courses under development.

Local learning incubator

Help librarians and community organizers produce new open web learning content.

Open content studio

Hack on open textbooks and help build a global courseware catalog.

Hackerspace playground

Learn how to make tech with processing.js, Arduino, and MakerBot.

Wikipedia lounge

Pitch in on projects that fuse Wikipedia into the world of learning.

Open source classroom

Convert open source projects into teaching resources in your classroom.

What's it all about?

The open nature of the internet is revolutionizing how we learn. Mozilla's 2010 Drumbeat Festival will gather teachers...

  • Learn how to organize a Peer 2 Peer University course.
  • Help build a 'digital backpack' to track credentials for online learners.
  • Trade ideas about teaching the open web.

Who should come?

  • Open textbook authors
  • People teaching web
  • Edupunks
  • Hackerspaces
  • Creative Commoners
  • Radical Librarians
  • And, of course, teachers and learners.
